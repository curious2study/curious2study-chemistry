What to Expect

This page will host the chemistry video library for Curious2Study, built on the OpenStax Chemistry 2e textbook. Video lectures, study guides, and links to the source material are being put together now.

Once the first set of videos is ready, this page will list each chapter with links to its lectures on YouTube, along with a way to report any errors you find.

  • Study guides summarizing key terms and concepts for each chapter
  • A link to the archived textbook PDF and the official OpenStax page
  • A simple way to flag problems with a video or study guide
